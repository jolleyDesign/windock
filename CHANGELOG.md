### Added

- A dock of pinned and running apps, with indicators for open windows, the focused app and apps
  asking for attention.
- Live window previews on hover: click one to switch to it, or close it with ✕ or a middle click.
- Magnification, a frosted-glass background, and a light and dark theme that follows Windows.
- Any screen edge and monitor, auto-hide, and an option to reserve space so maximized windows
  don't cover the dock.
- Drag to reorder, drag off to unpin, drop files onto an app to open them with it, or between apps
  to pin them. Taskbar pins can be imported.
- Keyboard shortcuts for pinned apps (Ctrl+Alt+1–9) and for showing the dock (Win+Shift+D).
- A settings window drawn on the CPU, and a hot-reloaded `config.toml`.
- An option to hide the Windows taskbar while the dock runs.
- A clock, and network, volume and battery icons, at the end of the dock or in a corner bar of
  their own. They open notifications and Quick Settings.
- Other apps' tray icons, with the ones Windows keeps off the taskbar in a grid behind an arrow.
  The grid stays open while an icon's menu is up.
- A Start button and a Show desktop button. Drag the Start button off the dock to hide it.
- A right-click menu drawn like the dock, with Close at the end of each app's menu.
